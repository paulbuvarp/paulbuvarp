{{- /* A lead image, sitting between the title and the standfirst. Front matter: hero: assets-relative path, e.g. img/hvem-eier-overfloden.jpg heroAlt: what it shows, for a reader who cannot see it. Required — the image is skipped without it, because an undescribed decorative image on an argumentative essay is worse than no image at all. heroCredit: the line beneath. Optional, but see the note below. Hugo resizes at build time and serves webp, with a jpeg fallback for the handful of clients that still need one, and srcset so a phone does not download a desktop-sized file. On the credit line: if the image was generated rather than photographed or commissioned, say so there. Not as a legal matter — as a practical one. This site belongs to someone who has had to rebut an accusation of AI authorship in public, and an unlabelled synthetic image is exactly the detail a second accusation would be built on. Labelled, it is evidence of the transparency the essays argue for. */ -}} {{- with .Params.hero -}} {{- $src := resources.Get . -}} {{- $alt := $.Params.heroAlt -}} {{- if and $src $alt -}} {{- $w1400 := $src.Resize "1400x webp q82" -}} {{- $w900 := $src.Resize "900x webp q82" -}} {{- $w600 := $src.Resize "600x webp q82" -}} {{- $jpg := $src.Resize "1400x jpg q82" -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
